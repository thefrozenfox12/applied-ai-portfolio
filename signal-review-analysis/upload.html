Processing and data handling

Each review is classified independently from its selected title and text. Binary mode returns positive or negative; three-class mode also permits neutral. Explicit star-rating phrases are masked before inference. These classifications do not measure accuracy without independently established labels.

Results expire 30 minutes after completion and are removed on the next batch/status request, or when the server restarts. At most 10 completed batches are retained. Export results before leaving. Review text is sent to the configured model provider, whose retention policy also applies. This workspace uses one shared access token and is intended for one owner or a trusted team.

Failed reviews remain marked as failed and are never counted as positive, negative, or neutral. Export the results to identify them, then upload those reviews in a new batch to retry.
